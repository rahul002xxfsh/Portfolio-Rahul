From Friction.
To One Checkout.

Pluxee meal cards get blocked the moment an online cart has one non-food item. I designed a split checkout where food goes on Pluxee and the rest on UPI or a card, in a single order.

Zeta: Pluxee Split Payments
Research · Market Sizing · MoSCoW · Wireframes · Rollout

Read The Case Study ↗

Rohan's checkout today

  • Adds groceries, dishwashing liquid, tissues and toothpaste to his Instamart cart
  • Goes to pay, and Pluxee isn't offered because of the non-food items
  • Has to remove the non-food items or pay the full ₹1,450 with UPI
  • His Pluxee balance stays untouched
BeforeAfter

After: ₹1,200 for food on Pluxee, ₹250 for the rest on UPI. One order, no items removed.
