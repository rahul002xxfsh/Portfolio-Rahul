Case Study: Elevating User Engagement on Indus App Store

1. Problem Statement

The Indus App Store, built to serve regional users across India, was facing key challenges around user engagement and app session duration. Despite a growing number of app installations, users were not spending enough time exploring new apps or features.

Common pain points included:

  • Low interaction with curated content and app recommendations.
  • Lack of engaging UI components to retain user attention.
  • Absence of a feedback loop through user ratings and reviews.
  • User demand for personalization, dark mode, and visual app discovery—features commonly found in global app marketplaces like Google Play.

To compete effectively and cater to diverse Indian audiences, the platform needed modern, high-impact features that would drive deeper usage, enhance discoverability, and build user trust.

2. Proposed Solution

To address these challenges, I led the product vision and roadmap for the following feature rollouts:

🟣 Short Videos for App Discovery

  • A swipeable short video module showcasing trending apps through 10–30 second previews.
  • Inspired by the reel format, allowing users to explore apps quickly with visual appeal.

🟢 User Ratings & Reviews

  • Enabled users to provide feedback on installed apps.
  • Integrated moderation workflows and visible rating summaries to build social proof.

⚫ Dark Mode

  • System-driven toggle to shift the entire UI into a night-friendly format.
  • Designed to improve comfort during evening use and reduce screen fatigue.

Each feature was aimed at improving a specific engagement metric while keeping accessibility and regional usability in mind.

3. Execution Strategy

🧠 Research & Discovery

  • Used Mixpanel and Google Analytics to track drop-offs, average session duration, and scroll depth.
  • Conducted qualitative feedback collection from users and internal stakeholders.
  • Benchmarked features against competing platforms and localization needs for regional audiences.

✍️ Product Planning

  • Created detailed PRDs, wireframes, and prototypes using Figma and internal CMS tools.
  • Mapped out cross-team dependencies with content, legal, QA, and engineering teams.
  • Prioritized sprints using Agile methodology, balancing short-term wins and long-term vision.

🚀 Feature Development

  • Introduced Generative AI workflows for faster prototyping and component reuse.
  • Led all Agile ceremonies: sprint planning, stand-ups, retrospectives, and UAT coordination.
  • Worked with content teams to localize Short Video content by state, language, and user preference.

4. Results & Business Impact

The rollout of these features delivered measurable impact:

FeatureResult
Short Videos2nd most-clicked homepage section within 30 days
Ratings & Reviews65% of new users submitted a review in the first 2 weeks
Dark ModeEnabled by 45% of daily active users post-launch
Overall📈 +30% increase in session duration 🔁 +20% boost in user retention

The features significantly improved app stickiness, increased discovery interaction, and gave users a voice in app quality assessment.

5. Cross-Team Collaboration

  • Content Team: Collaborated to curate app recommendations and Short Video playlists tailored to regions like Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka.
  • Engineering: Coordinated timelines, testing, and rollout plans across backend and Android app teams.
  • Design: Co-created low-fi to hi-fi prototypes aligned with brand guidelines and user accessibility.
  • QA & Legal: Ensured review compliance, dark mode rendering, and regional guidelines.

🏁 Conclusion

Through the strategic rollout of Short Videos, User Ratings & Reviews, and Dark Mode, I successfully transformed the Indus App Store from a functional app listing platform into an engaging, personalized, and user-first ecosystem. These features not only aligned with user expectations in a competitive app store landscape but also delivered tangible improvements in key product metrics like session duration and retention.
