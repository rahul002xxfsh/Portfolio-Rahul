UdChalo: Favourite Product Teardown. A travel booking platform for defence personnel and their families, and the improvements I would prioritise to make booking simpler and safer.

RoleAssociate Product Manager (product analysis)
ProductUdChalo, travel booking for defence personnel
FocusUser pain points, prioritisation, success metrics, risks

1. Product Overview

UdChalo is a travel booking platform tailored for defence personnel and their families. It offers exclusive discounts on flights, trains, buses and other services, with the aim of simplifying travel planning while honouring the defence community.

2. Why I Like the Product

  • It solves a niche problem for a clearly defined user group.
  • It creates a sense of belonging and recognition.
  • It simplifies travel booking while integrating unique benefits.

3. User Pain Points

  • Lack of transparent, exclusive flight discounts for defence personnel.
  • Tax filing prompts appear unnecessarily across multiple sections.
  • Limited personalisation of offers and deals.
  • Verification of defence relatives and families is weak and prone to misuse.
  • Seat selection is buried deep in menus instead of being part of the booking flow.
  • The "Fauji Family" feature feels redundant and underused.

4. Proposed Improvements

  • Streamline seat selection into the booking flow.
  • Limit the tax filing section to relevant areas only.
  • Remove irrelevant deals and leverage partner offers (credit cards, banks), similar to MakeMyTrip.
  • Introduce a robust verification system for dependents and families to reduce misuse.
  • Replace "Fauji Family" with more impactful features such as bundled family travel offers.
  • Improve sorting options (Cheapest to Fastest flights) with a toggle-based UI.

5. Prioritisation Framework

PriorityInitiative
🟢 High impact, low effortSeat selection redesign, improved sorting options
🟡 High impact, medium effortVerification system for dependents
🟡 Medium impact, low effortDeal curation with credit card and bank partnerships
🔴 Low impact, high effortRedesign of the "Fauji Family" feature

6. Success Metrics

  • Increase in retention rate of defence users.
  • Higher conversion rate from search to booking.
  • Improved CSAT / NPS on the booking flow.
  • Reduction in customer support queries about seat selection or tax filing.

7. Risks

RiskDetail
Legal / complianceA verification system may raise privacy and data concerns.
OperationalIntegrating with multiple banks and credit cards for deals.
User adoptionRemoving or altering existing features such as Fauji Family may confuse loyal users.
